01 / ABOUT RILEY

Systems that feel good.

A computer science graduate and gameplay programmer with a particular interest in cars, physics, and systems-driven gameplay.

Most of Riley's work centers around vehicle behavior: acceleration, braking, steering, traction, suspension, collisions, AI traffic, and the systems that make a vehicle actually feel good to drive.

She enjoys taking systems that look complicated under the hood and turning them into gameplay that feels simple, responsive, and satisfying. When something feels "off," she likes breaking it down until she finds the exact system responsible.
