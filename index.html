ABOUT

Systems first. Feel second.

Complex gameplay systems should disappear into the experience. The player should feel the result, not the code behind it.

Riley is a computer science graduate and gameplay programmer focused on cars, physics, and systems-driven gameplay. Most of her work centers around vehicle behavior: acceleration, braking, steering, traction, suspension, collisions, AI traffic, and systems that make a vehicle feel good to drive.

She likes taking complex systems and turning them into gameplay that feels simple, responsive, and satisfying. She also enjoys breaking down anything that feels "off" to find the exact system responsible.
