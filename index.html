01 / ABOUT

From idea to playable.

Riley enjoys working across multiple parts of development—not just programming, but the systems, spaces, and interactions that make a game feel alive.

Riley started with an interest in games and gradually became curious about how they were made. That curiosity grew into experimenting with her own projects and learning how to turn rough ideas into playable experiences.

Her focus includes gameplay programming, game systems, level design, and interactive experiences, with development spanning Unreal Engine and Roblox.
